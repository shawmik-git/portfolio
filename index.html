About Me

A little about my journey and who I am

My coding journey began alongside my Mathematics Honours studies. I was drawn to how logic and structure show up in both fields, and I started with JavaScript and TypeScript, then moved on to building full stack apps. I also write Fortran for numerical methods such as Newton–Raphson, which keeps my fundamentals sharp.

I enjoy building clean, practical products: community platforms, dashboards and tools that make everyday tasks easier. I like concise code, clear APIs and interfaces that feel effortless on every screen.

Away from the keyboard, I ride my bike around Jashore, tinker with PC hardware and home networking, play games and read manga. I’m curious, patient with hard problems, and always learning.
